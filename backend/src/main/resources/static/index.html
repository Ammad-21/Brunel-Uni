Why EcoMove?

About Us

EcoMove is a student-led project that turns everyday transport data into climate insights. Log trips, track your emissions, and experiment with cleaner routes.

Community

Join your classmates, friends, and local residents on shared challenges. Compare stats, unlock streaks, and celebrate collective CO₂ savings.

Challenges

Weekly goals push you to try greener options: bike-to-campus days, car-free weekends, or public transport streaks. Climb the leaderboard while cutting emissions.
